**Where the game is right now, in one paragraph.** Tallow Reach is a three.js space game played
from one built HTML file. You fly out of Meridian Outpost, take contracts, mine asteroids, fight
Newbie Raiders, and dock to spend the money. Ships are built out of **modules in bays** on the
Outfitting screen: a powerplant (10 MJ/s), a power cell (100 MJ), thrusters (150 m/s and 69 deg/s
on the basic set, 20 MJ boost that doubles the drive), shields (10 Shield HP, 1 a second back after a
4-second lull) and now **basic cannons** — 1 damage a shot, 3 shots a second, 2 MJ/s a gun while
the trigger is held, fittable in **either** primary bay. Everything that uses power draws it from
the same budget and refuses to run when the ship cannot pay. The **basic hull** grants 10 Hull HP, and no hull module fitted means no Hull HP at all — any hit reaching the hull destroys
the ship. Hostiles are **Newbie Raiders**: 10
Hull HP, no shields.

## How the module pattern works (copy this for the next part)

1. A part is one entry in `MODULES` in `src/data/modules.js`: `g` (which bay group), `n`, `d`, and
   the stat fields that group uses.
2. A system owns the translation from modules to numbers and keeps it in `state`:
   `sim/power.js` → `state.power`, `sim/drive.js` → `state.drive`, `sim/shield.js` →
   `state.shield`, `sim/guns.js` → `state.guns`. Each has a `refresh*()` that is called after a
   fit change (`sim/fitting.js`), a ship change (`sim/economy.js`), a save load (`ui/menu.js`) and
   dock/launch (`sim/session.js`). **Add your refresh to all four places**, or the ship keeps the
   old numbers.
3. A system that burns power calls `setDraw("<name>", mjps)` every frame and asks `hasPower()`
   before it does its work. A new name must exist in `state.power.use` first.
4. The UI reads `state`, never modules: `ui/hud.js` for the flight readouts, `ui/screens.js` for
   the outpost panels (`moduleStat(m)` gives the one-line number in a bay).
