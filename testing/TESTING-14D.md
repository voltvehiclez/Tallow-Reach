# 14d — wormhole systems

1. Open World. On a fresh default galaxy the first S0001 entrance is in **Harrowcross**. Press **M**, inspect Harrowcross, set a destination there and travel there normally. Open its pocket map and warp to **System Wormhole · S0001**. On older/advanced worlds the entrance may have moved.
2. Fly close to the grey sphere and press **R**. The system line should change from the regular system name to **S0001**. Look for the star or black hole; check which side of the rocks is lit.
3. Press **M**. It should open on S0001, showing its core, Belt, Wrecks and exit. Click **View regular space**, then **Return to S0001**. Neither should move the ship. Warp to the Belt; save, reload and check the name, position and map again.
4. Fly back through the live exit with **R**: it should lead to the original entrance. Re-enter. Spend 20 minutes of unpaused flight time inside. After expiry, warp to a new live System Wormhole pocket labelled with a regular-space system and press R. You should arrive at its new regular-space partner, not lose cargo, die or be stuck.
5. Check an old save still opens at its saved location. Simple wormholes and rifts should still show **Uncharted Space**, not an S-number.

6. Inside an S-system, check for the grey wormhole sky and grey ambient fill. Inspect the local chart: there should be two to four live doors. Fly through a door labelled with another S-number, then go back through its partner; verify both HUD names. Take a regular-space exit and check that the host sky and light return.
